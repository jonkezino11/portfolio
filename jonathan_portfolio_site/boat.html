Overview

The project goal was to design and build an autonomous robotic boat capable of navigating through water while maintaining stability, supporting onboard electronics, and integrating propulsion, steering, and embedded control.

My primary contributions were the SolidWorks design of the hull and keel, design iteration, component-placement decisions, and ESP32 programming in C++.

Design Requirements

Reduce hydrodynamic drag

Maintain stability

Package electronics safely

Support propulsion and steering

Manage weight distribution

Remain manufacturable

System Integration

The mechanical layout was developed around the interaction between the hull, keel, battery, ESP32, propulsion motor, steering mechanism, wiring, and internal component placement.

Controls

ESP32 → Propulsion / Steering → Boat Motion

I programmed the ESP32 microcontroller in C++ to control propulsion and steering, allowing the mechanical platform to respond to autonomous control commands.

What I Learned

  • Mechanical design must account for electronics, controls, and packaging from the beginning.
  • Weight distribution and component placement significantly affect real-world stability.
  • Iteration is necessary when testing exposes integration and performance issues.
